My short story

I want to start this book with a simple truth: being a student is not easy. Your time is divided between studies, family, friends, and your own dreams. On top of that, limited pocket money or financial pressure makes life even harder. I’ve been through this myself. I know the feeling of wanting to support yourself, to carry your own expenses, but at the same time making sure your income is halal and full of barakah.

I was just a normal student. My day was filled with classes, assignments, and the usual student struggles. But one thing always bothered me: money. My pocket money was limited. Sometimes it covered the basics, but not the little things I wanted — like extra books, courses, or even just saving up for something personal. And I didn’t like asking my parents for more. I wanted to be independent, even if just a little.

Then I discovered the idea of ebooks. At first, I honestly thought: “Who’s going to buy a PDF?” But the more I learned, the more I realized this was something real: a halal way to share knowledge and earn money at the same time. I’m still at the beginning of my journey — I haven’t made my first sale yet. But I know one thing: this is the right path. That’s why I decided to write this guide. Because I know there are thousands of students like me, starting from zero, with limited money but unlimited potential.
